# Account & Data Deletion — MealTime

**App:** MealTime
**Developer:** Kartik Khandelwal
**Contact:** kartikkhandelwal1234589@gmail.com
**Last updated: May 28, 2026**

This page explains how to request deletion of your **MealTime** account and the
data associated with it.

---

## How to delete your account

### Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app (instant)
1. Open the **MealTime** app and make sure you are signed in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account**.
4. Confirm when prompted (you may be asked to re-authenticate with Google or
   your password for security).

Your account and synced data are removed immediately.

### Option 2 — Request deletion by email
If you can no longer access the app, email
**kartikkhandelwal1234589@gmail.com** from the address associated with your
account, with the subject **"Delete my MealTime account."** We will verify your
request and process it within **30 days**.

---

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is **permanently removed**:

- Your **authentication record** (email address and/or Google sign-in identity,
  user ID).
- Your **synced content** stored in our Cloud Firestore database: favorite
  recipes, AI-generated recipes, shopping-list items, and your dietary/theme
  preferences.
- All data stored **locally on your device** for the app is removed when you
  uninstall the app.

## What data is kept, and for how long

- **Crash logs** (Firebase Crashlytics) are retained for up to **90 days**.
- **Aggregated, anonymized usage and analytics data** (Firebase Analytics) is
  retained for up to **14 months** per Google's defaults. This data is not tied
  to your account after deletion and cannot be used to identify you.
- We may retain limited records where required to comply with legal
  obligations; these are deleted once no longer required.
